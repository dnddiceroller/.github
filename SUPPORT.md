# Support

## Using the roller

Questions about [dnddiceroller.com](https://www.dnddiceroller.com) itself (rolling, themes, settings, your account) go to the contact page on the site, or dev@dnddiceroller.com.

## Checking a receipt

1. Open the **certificate** link in your roll log. It checks the hash against the beacon in your browser.
2. Still unsure? Run [verify-receipt.mjs](https://github.com/dnddiceroller/examples) yourself.
3. Read what a receipt proves in [roll-verification-spec](https://github.com/dnddiceroller/roll-verification-spec/blob/main/SPEC.md).

A receipt that does not verify is worth an issue. Paste the certificate link. It holds only public record.
